# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

This folder contains the core project management guidance used by OctoAcme to plan, deliver, and improve cross-functional work. The documents here are intended to give teams a shared understanding of how work is initiated, prioritized, communicated, and released, while also providing a consistent foundation for onboarding and collaboration.

OctoAcme's approach follows a simple lifecycle: validate the need and define success during initiation, plan scope and dependencies, execute in short, well-structured increments, release with checks and rollback readiness, and then reflect with retrospectives to improve the process. The project management model emphasizes clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and decision points that keep delivery aligned with business goals. These practices are designed to reduce ambiguity, improve team coordination, and support predictable execution across projects.

The operating model includes defined personas and responsibilities to support accountability and efficiency. Product leads define what success looks like, project managers coordinate schedules, communications, and risks, developers deliver the work, and QA validates quality against acceptance criteria. Stakeholders and leadership provide input and approval at key checkpoints, while the team maintains transparency through regular updates and clear escalation paths. This structure helps the organization balance speed with quality and ensures that roles remain understandable for both new and experienced contributors.

Communication is treated as a key part of delivery rather than an afterthought. OctoAcme recommends regular standups, weekly syncs, stakeholder updates, and issue escalation when blockers or business-impacting risks arise. Quality is also built into the workflow through definitions of done, pull request reviews, CI checks, testing standards, and release readiness validations. After each major milestone or sprint, teams review outcomes, capture lessons learned, and convert those insights into action items that improve future execution. Together, these practices create a repeatable project management system that is practical, scalable, and continuously improving.
