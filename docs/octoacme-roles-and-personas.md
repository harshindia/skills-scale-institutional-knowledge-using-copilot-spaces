# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, ensure architectural consistency, and maintain code quality standards. They collaborate with Product and Project Leads to translate requirements into scalable, maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Define or review technical designs for major features
- Ensure adherence to architectural patterns and coding standards
- Mentor developers and facilitate technical discussions
- Identify technical debt and propose mitigation strategies
- Contribute to technology selection and infrastructure decisions

### Goals
- Maintain system scalability, reliability, and maintainability
- Reduce technical risk and enable faster delivery cycles
- Foster a culture of continuous learning and code excellence

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical spikes
- Code review comments and pull request feedback
- Architecture decision records (ADRs) and tech talks

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with **Developers** to guide implementation and ensure architectural alignment
- Partners with **Product Managers** to translate feature requirements into technical designs
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** to identify and manage technical dependencies and risks
- Collaborates with **QA Lead** on testability and quality standards

---

## QA Lead / Quality Assurance Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the quality strategy and testing approach. They define quality standards, design test plans, validate acceptance criteria, and build testing infrastructure to ensure products meet customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria frameworks
- Design and manage test strategies (unit, integration, end-to-end, smoke tests)
- Validate that features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Build and maintain automated testing infrastructure
- Identify and triage quality issues and regressions

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, high-quality products to customers
- Enable teams to ship with confidence
- Reduce defects reaching production

### Typical Communication
- Quality metrics and test reports
- Test plans and acceptance criteria reviews
- Release readiness assessments

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with **Developers** to establish testing practices and review test coverage
- Aligns with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and quality expectations
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** on testing timelines and release quality gates
- Partners with **Technical Lead** to ensure architectural testability
- Collaborates with **Release Manager** on deployment readiness and smoke test execution

---

## Release Manager / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, manage release schedules, maintain deployment automation, and ensure smooth production operations. They serve as the bridge between development and production environments.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release planning and deployment schedules
- Maintain and improve deployment automation (CI/CD pipelines)
- Ensure rollback and incident response procedures are ready
- Monitor deployment health and post-release stability
- Document release notes and deployment procedures

### Goals
- Enable rapid, safe, and repeatable deployments
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and downtime
- Provide visibility into release status and production health

### Typical Communication
- Release notes and deployment schedules
- Deployment checklists and runbooks
- Post-deployment status reports and metrics

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with **Developers** to understand deployment dependencies and enable smooth rollouts
- Aligns with **Product Managers** on release timing and feature prioritization for deployments
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** on release schedules and communication plans
- Partners with **Technical Lead** on infrastructure and deployment architecture decisions
- Collaborates with **QA Lead** on smoke test execution and deployment validation
